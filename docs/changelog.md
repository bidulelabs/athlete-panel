# Changelog

Use the documentation link for the version you installed. Each release page includes its deployment bundle and verification report.

## Unreleased

- Race analysis estimates from the moving time of your reference efforts: every stretch of a GPX or FIT slower than about 1 km/h that climbs or descends less than 180 m an hour counts as a pause and is left out. References already imported are read again from their kept file, with no step to take. A time you entered by hand is kept as entered; the field is now called **Moving time**, so leave pauses out of a new one.
- **Races** gains a **Scenarios** tab between Race day and Nutrition. It lists the race's scenarios with their effort and weather assumption, their stops and their expected finish, marks one **Outdated** when the course or the plan's aid stations changed since it was saved, and deletes one after confirmation (undo from History). Creating, comparing and applying scenarios come later. Nothing new is stored.
- Training stores race scenarios: alternatives to a race plan with their own effort, weather and aid station stops, up to four per race. Deleting a race deletes its scenarios, the confirmation says so, and one undo restores them. No screen shows them yet, and an assistant never reads or writes them. Upgrade the application and CLI together; to roll back, restore the backup taken before the upgrade, because an older version cannot read a change history holding one.
- A race plan can store an effort and a weather assumption as percentages of the moving time, with a note. **Race day** gains **Change effort and weather**: Race day, Nutrition and the race card then use the adjusted finish and moving time, the race card and its downloaded file say `Effort +8 % · weather +6 % (your assumption)`, and Course keeps the unadjusted finish. An assistant never reads or writes them. Upgrade the application and CLI together; to roll back, restore the backup taken before the upgrade, because an older version cannot read a plan holding them.
- Race day, Nutrition and the race card add the planned stops to the moving time from the course estimate instead of counting them inside it. A plan with stops now finishes later by its total of stops; the arrival at a station counts only the stops of the stations before it; the notice about stops longer than the estimate is gone.
- Race analysis keeps an unsaved terrain draft saveable after you add, edit or remove a reference effort in the same tab; a change from another tab still requires reviewing saved data first. Reference efforts no longer return the redundant `comparison` field.
- Training stores one race plan per race: the owner's target, a briefing, pacing by course section, assumptions and open questions. **Connect an agent** offers a **Race to prepare**: the agent of that connection reads the race's plan and a summary of its course (no coordinates or file names) and proposes the plan through the usual owner review, in the same proposal as phases and workouts; the target stays the owner's. Deleting a race deletes its plan, and one undo restores both. **Races** gains a **Preparation** tab to write the plan by hand and to see when a new course or target calls for a review, with the weeks left until race day (phase, target, planned sessions). See [Race preparation](features/race-preparation.md).
- **Settings** gains a **Nutrition** tab: the gels, drinks and food you use with their carbohydrate, fluid, sodium and caffeine per serving (unknown values stay unknown), your tolerance of each, how much you can carry and your diet preferences. An agent reads them only through a connection limited to a race; other connections and historical review do not receive them. See [Race preparation](features/race-preparation.md#nutrition-in-settings).

- A race plan can hold a start time, a finish cutoff and aid stations (distance, cutoff, planned stop, water, food, drop bag, action, source, confirmed), set by the owner or proposed by an assistant connected for the race. Assistants limited to a race read them in that race's plan.

- **Races** gains a **Race day** tab: the expected finish as a clock time, moving time, planned stops and finish cutoff; the start time and finish cutoff; aid stations in course order with arrival ranges, cutoff margins, the leg before each one and whether it is confirmed; and a window to add, edit or remove a station. See [Race preparation](features/race-preparation.md).

- **Races** gains a **Nutrition** tab: targets per hour for carbohydrate, fluid and sodium (unset targets read "unknown"), free texts for the days before, breakfast and recovery, and your diet preferences from Settings. The race plan stores them. Assistants limited to a race see them in that race's plan. See [Race preparation](features/race-preparation.md#nutrition-in-the-race-plan).

- **Nutrition** in **Races** gains the food plan during the race: products from Settings with servings and how often, and cues in your own words, edited in one window. It shows what each intake brings with the product's tolerance, what the plan gives per hour beside each target, the carbohydrate over the moving time when the course has an estimate, and which products are left out for an unknown value or because they were removed from Settings.
- Training stores fueling rehearsals of a race. Deleting a race, a session (once its completion is cleared) or a plan holding a rehearsed session also deletes its rehearsals, the confirmation says so, and one undo restores them. Agents cannot write rehearsals, and a proposal that would delete one is refused.
- **Nutrition** in **Races** lists the race's fueling rehearsals and logs one on a session completed or partial in the last 12 weeks: the servings planned from the race's food plan and those taken, plain water, the stomach, the conditions and what to keep or change. An empty field is never counted as 0. Each rehearsal can be edited, its session locked, or deleted.

- **Race day** shows what to carry on each leg row: the water and products to leave with, computed from the moving time, your nutrition schedule and targets, your carry capacity and your drop bags. A **What to carry** block shows what each drop bag holds and lacks, warns when a leg needs more water than you can carry, and says when the water need or the capacity is unknown. Without leg times nothing is shown. The station window now edits the drop bag with products from Settings. Nothing new is stored.

- **Races** gains a **Race card** tab: the expected finish, your briefing, breakfast, each aid station with its arrival range, what to do and what to leave with, the cues, the food line and the recovery, on one page. **Print** keeps only the card, and **Download offline copy** saves one HTML file with no script and no link, marked with the plan revision and the download date. Nothing new is stored.
- An assistant connected for a race can now propose the start time, the finish cutoff, aid stations, the hourly targets, the food schedule, the cues and the day texts of that race's plan, and, when you approve a new connection for a race, reads that race's fueling rehearsals. A connection approved before this release keeps its context and does not receive rehearsals until you approve it again. Review shows each of them readably, with product names, and hides empty fields of a new plan. The assistant never confirms a station: a station it adds starts not confirmed, and renaming or moving a confirmed station clears its confirmation. A drop bag or food schedule naming a product that is not in your nutrition profile is refused. The planned servings of a rehearsal now use the same intake clock as **What to carry**.

### Upgrade steps

Earlier releases cannot read a change history holding a fueling rehearsal: see [rehearsal upgrade guidance](development.md#rehearsal-upgrade). Earlier releases cannot read race plans holding aid stations or nutrition: see [race day upgrade guidance](development.md#race-day-upgrade) and [race nutrition upgrade guidance](development.md#race-nutrition-upgrade). Earlier releases cannot read stored race plans, grants limited to a race or race plan proposals: rollback after those records exist requires the matching pre-upgrade backup. See [race plan upgrade guidance](development.md#race-plan-upgrade).

Earlier releases cannot read a profile holding nutrition: rollback after it is saved requires the matching pre-upgrade backup. See [nutrition profile upgrade guidance](development.md#nutrition-profile-upgrade).

## v0.5.0 (2026-11-13)

- Training plans groups a plan's weeks into phases (base, build, peak, taper, recovery), each with an optional focus such as "VO2max" and optional weekly hours, distance and climbing targets that move in a straight line from the first week to the last; each week's target sits beside its planned total, can be set by hand and reset to calculated; a plan starting midweek shows calendar weeks apart from its own weeks. Phases stand on calendar dates, so one season can span several plans: moving a plan asks whether its own phases move with it, names any phase shared with another plan, which stays, and duplicating says phases are not copied. Charts draws a phase target as a band behind Training hours, Training by sport and Weekly climbing, with a Plan phases period that reaches future weeks. The Calendar week header names each hours figure (`Target 8–10 h · Planned 9 h · Recorded 7 h`), showing only those that exist, and keeps the target in place of No plan when a week has no workouts. Agents read the phases that touch their planning dates and, when a new connection allows phase changes (checked by default), propose phases within their proposal dates alongside the workouts they frame, for your review. Ask an agent to “Detail the whole plan” or to “Outline the season and detail the next two weeks”; either way you review one proposal. No SQL migration; upgrade web and worker together and reload open browser tabs.
- The default Docker worker now reaps orphaned child processes, preventing zombie accumulation from exhausting its process limit and breaking health checks. Managed installs receive the setting through the normal coordinated upgrade; adopted installs must first reconcile `init: true` into their saved worker configuration. See [worker process-reaping upgrade steps](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.5.0/docs/development.md#worker-process-reaping). Existing health checks and process limits are preserved; no database migration is required for this fix.

- Optional Race analysis links calendar races to uploaded GPX courses, synchronized route/elevation views, smoothed climbing, terrain settings and finish-time planning ranges from GPX/FIT reference efforts. Missing measurements and insufficient evidence remain explicit. Files stay on your server; this adds no Garmin GPS import or agent access. Activate the matching composition with the new Race analysis migration and refreshed runtime schema grants through the [coordinated upgrade procedure](development.md#coordinated-upgrades-and-plugin-activation), rebuild/restart web and worker, and reload browser tabs. Keep a matched pre-upgrade backup for rollback; disabling the plugin retains its files and analysis.

- Race analysis rejects malformed files without unbounded pattern matching and flags reference times outside its running/hiking model. Planning ranges also account for climbing and duration extrapolation. Reloading after a conflict keeps an edit's original version, so retrying cannot silently overwrite another tab. These refinements require the matching application rebuild and browser reload, with no additional SQL migration.

- Calendar accessibility: screen readers announce each day with its full date and the empty-day button with the short date. The first-workout message is a second-level heading under the page title. Shared buttons, toggles, switches, badges and tabs animate only color, border, focus ring and press movement. Rebuild/restart the application and reload browser tabs; no SQL migration or new permissions are required.

- Calendar date navigation stays visible while scrolling, with sticky desktop weekday labels aligned to the day columns. Desktop days offer **+ Add** below existing cards on hover or keyboard focus; phones retain their visible day add action. Today and date jumps reveal the requested day after its range loads. Rebuild/restart and reload browser tabs; no migration or configuration change is required.

### Upgrade steps

Earlier releases cannot read grants carrying the plan phase permission or phase proposals: rollback after those records exist requires the matching pre-upgrade backup. See [plan phase proposal upgrade guidance](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.5.0/docs/development.md#plan-phase-proposal-upgrade).

## v0.4.0 (2026-11-12)

- Removed the redundant Cycling & sleep page. The new Printable training week example prints planned sessions, intervals and strength exercises, with optional notes and browser PDF export. It ships disabled by default, and the Plugins page’s **Create with agent** dialog now suggests this example. Follow the [example activation guide](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md#printable-training-week-example) to enable it. Upgrades removing Cycling & sleep require the normal reviewed plugin-removal acknowledgement; no SQL migration or athlete-data deletion is involved.

- Calendar cards explicitly show completion, Garmin links, recorded results and planned values, including partial sessions performed on another day. Sea glass card colors, subtle status edges, emphasized recorded values and a separated source footer make completed and linked sessions easier to scan in both themes. Selection keeps an orange outline while preserving the status tint. Unlinked activities offer Link workout; narrower desktops use a drawer so calendar columns stay readable. New Garmin imports automatically link unique day/sport matches, respecting subtypes and replacing manual measurements while retaining status and annotations. Previously stored activities remain unchanged; newly imported older sessions are eligible, and automatic links can be undone. The inspector explains what Unlink clears and how to use Undo to restore a replaced manual log. Apply the additive Garmin staging and host audit-index migrations with the matched web/worker and composition through the [coordinated upgrade procedure](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md#coordinated-upgrades-and-plugin-activation), then reload browser tabs. Rollback after new completion metadata is saved requires the matching pre-upgrade backup.

- Agent workspace now focuses on Proposals and Connected agents, with Change history in a side panel and a consistently prominent orange Connect an agent button. Escape closes Undo confirmations while keeping either history panel open, including after a confirmation-backdrop click. Undo errors stay visible below the history header when reviewing later entries. Compact proposal previews show affected dates; full summaries remain visible in review. Permissions expand on demand, inactive connections start collapsed, and disconnecting explains its effect on pending proposals. Larger reviews defer their rows until expanded; filtering opens matching groups immediately for up to 200 matches and keeps broader results collapsed, and Apply names the complete change count. Rebuild/restart the application and reload browser tabs; no SQL migration, permission change or CLI update is required.

- Agent proposal review distinguishes workouts, races, goals and notes with labeled icons and theme-aware colors. The plan/proposal summary is visible at the top by default on desktop and phones. Review controls remain visible while scrolling, new items have simpler details, and search/type/action filters keep whole-proposal application explicit. Date search accepts the labels shown in the review as well as ISO dates. Applied proposals offer calendar navigation and undo; keyboard focus returns to Review when the dialog closes. Calendar goals retain their blue target treatment. Rebuild/restart the application and reload browser tabs; no SQL migration or new permissions are required.

- Charts adds six templates: Training hours, Training by sport, Workout heart rate, Weekly climbing, Resting heart rate and HRV, and Estimated training load. Training hours, Training by sport and Weekly climbing support selected sports grouped by day, week or month. Planned and recorded hours use the same filter; empty results identify the sport selection, and partial periods and missing measurements remain labelled. Sport bars remain visible in long daily views on phones. Arrow keys move the chart tooltip even while the mouse pointer rests on the chart. Activate Charts with the matching web/worker build through the [coordinated upgrade procedure](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md#coordinated-upgrades-and-plugin-activation), then reload open browser tabs; no SQL migration or athlete-data conversion is required.
- Agents can propose new races and dated goals, alone or with a training plan. New planning connections enable this by default with an option to turn it off; existing connections require fresh approval. You still review and apply every proposal, and duplicate events are blocked, including identical accented titles with different Unicode encodings.
- Health opens on the last 30 days. You can choose 7, 30 or 90 days and browse earlier dates without moving your planning calendar.
- Activities labels duration and distance totals as partial when measurements are missing. Each total shows how many activities lack that measurement.
- Garmin activity imports preserve elevation loss, maximum heart rate and time in five heart-rate zones when Garmin supplies them.
- Agent workspace lists pending proposals first and keeps applied and rejected proposals in a collapsible history.
- If only activity or health records change while an agent prepares a training proposal, you can review those changes alongside the proposal. You must acknowledge the differences before applying it.
- GitHub release pages show what changed and how to upgrade before the fresh install instructions.
- A workout interval can end when you press the lap button instead of after a time or a distance, for example an open warm-up. Workout details show it as “Until lap button”, and agents can propose it. Steps saved this way cannot be read by an older release, so roll back with the matching pre-upgrade backup.

### Upgrade steps

If an agent read planning context before the upgrade and still has an unsubmitted draft, have it read the context again before submitting.

For event proposals, update application and agent CLI source together and reload browser tabs. Use the normal coordinated upgrade procedure to record the new plugin composition; no SQL migration is added. Earlier releases cannot read grants carrying the new event permission or event proposals: rollback after those records exist requires the matching pre-upgrade backup. See [event proposal upgrade guidance](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/development.md#event-proposal-upgrade).

## v0.3.0 (2026-11-02)

- Managed upgrades check public HTTPS access before entering maintenance. Deployment health requests identify themselves to proxies such as Cloudflare and explain HTTP 403 rejections.
- Owners can grant agents up to five calendar years of history reads and a separate planning period of up to one calendar year. Training changes still require owner review.
- Agent planning context includes daily health data for the latest seven days and weekly averages with measurement counts for earlier days in the approved period.
- Garmin continues scheduling recent imports after a failure and clears the retry status after a successful recent import.
- Editing a profile preserves fields you leave out. Editing a workout preserves its duration when you leave that field untouched.
