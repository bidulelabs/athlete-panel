# Feature: Charts

## Purpose and scope

The athlete sees how training built up over weeks and months, starting from a familiar chart template instead of a blank chart builder. The first template, **Training hours**, compares planned and recorded hours per week and marks the races entered in the calendar. **Training by sport** splits the same recorded hours by sport. **Workout heart rate** shows the average heart rate of each session, one sport at a time. **Weekly climbing** shows recorded elevation gain per sport and how steep running was. **Resting heart rate and HRV** compares each night with its 7-night average and a usual range.

Outside scope: other templates (each arrives in its own change, see [issue #79](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/issues/79)), arbitrary data-category selection, an **Add metric** control, readiness scores, workload ratios and pace or power curves. Selecting a chart never grants an agent access or sends data anywhere.

## Intended behavior

Specification status: approved scope for the template picker and six templates, plus sport selection and day/week/month grouping for the three volume templates.
The owner approved the shared sport filters and grouping described below on 2026-11-05 in the implementation chat. This extends the initial template-only scope; the broader Add metric builder remains separate.

Requirement source: [issue #79](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/issues/79), proposal by the maintainer and agreement of 2026-11-04 (built-in plugin, shadcn Charts, dedicated read-only summary request, small changes in the stated order).

- **Charts** is a built-in, optional plugin with one page. It opens on a template list; choosing a template opens that chart, and **All templates** returns to the list.
- **Training hours** shows recorded hours as bars and planned hours as a dashed line, per week by default. Each bucket containing a `race` event gets a vertical line in the event colour with its title; goals and other events are not drawn.
- Weeks are the Calendar's weeks: the same week start preference, the same planned and recorded duration totals (the Calendar [weekly summary](training-workspace.md) calculation, run on the server for every week), and the same duration format.
- **Period** offers the last 4, 12 or 52 weeks ending with the current week, **This season**, or custom dates. This season runs from the start date of the active training plan to the current week; a plan is active when today falls between its start date and its last planned day, and the latest start wins when several are. Without an active plan (none, not started yet, or already ended), the season starts on 1 January. A season longer than 53 weeks shows the same error as custom dates. Custom dates expand to whole weeks and accept at most 53 weeks; an end date before the start date shows an error and sends no request.
- **Shared volume controls:** Training hours, Training by sport and Weekly climbing offer **Sports** (all, one, or several of the existing six chart sport groups) and **Group by** (day, week, month). They default to All sports and Week. The sport picker supports keyboard and touch, offers an Only action per sport and All sports reset, and keeps at least one selected. Choices are transient and reset when leaving the template, like the existing period choice. Other includes unresolved activities and sport groups outside the five named categories; subgroups count once under their group.
- The existing period selection still expands custom dates to Calendar weeks and stays capped at 53 weeks. Changing sports or grouping never changes those dates. Weeks keep the athlete's Calendar week boundary. Days follow recorded local/performed dates. Months use calendar months clipped to the selected range; clipped months read **partial**. The bucket containing today reads **in progress**; buckets after today read **Upcoming**. A monthly view does not expand the request to full months or fabricate additional data.
- Both recorded and planned hours use the same selected sports. Training by sport keeps its fixed colors and shows only selected recorded sports. Elevation gain is summed for the selected sports. Running climb appears only when Running is selected, and divides the sum of eligible running ascent by the sum of its matching distances across the bucket; it never averages weekly ratios. Races remain date markers, independent of sport selection because events have no sport field. Titles, axis dates, captions, tooltips and View values use the same grouping and filtered data.
- Workout heart rate retains its existing single-sport/session view. Resting heart rate and HRV retain nightly readings. Estimated training load retains all-sport daily load and its fixed 7-/42-day averages. Those three templates do not accept the new summary input controls.
- A day, week or month with no selected recorded session shows no bar and reads **No sessions recorded**, as in the Calendar. A session measured at zero minutes reads `0m`. A bucket whose recorded sessions all lack a duration shows no bar and **Duration missing**. A partly measured bucket shows the known sum with how many sessions lack a duration. A bucket without planned sessions has no point on the planned line and reads **No plan**.
- The current day, week or month is marked **in progress**: its bar is drawn as an outline. Periods after today show no recorded value and read **Upcoming**.
- Sport bars for past periods use solid fills without a background-colored border, so narrow daily bars remain visible in long periods on phones. The current period retains its sport-colored outline.
- **Training by sport** stacks the recorded hours of each selected day, week or month by sport, in a fixed order from the baseline: running, cycling, winter sports, hiking, swimming, then **Other** on top. Other gathers every other sport group and every activity whose sport could not be identified. Each sport keeps its colour whatever the period; the legend and table list only sports recorded in the period. A subgroup (trail running) counts once, under its sport group. The planned line, races, current week outline and **Upcoming** weeks are the same as in Training hours. Per sport, a sport without sessions that week reads **No sessions recorded**, a session measured at zero reads `0m`, and missing durations follow the Training hours rules.
- **Workout heart rate** draws one dot per recorded session of the chosen sport (the Calendar's recorded sessions: imported activities, and manually logged workouts on their performed date; a workout linked to an activity counts once, as that activity), at its date and its average heart rate in bpm. **Sport** lists the chart sports with at least one session with heart rate in the period and starts on the one with the most. Sessions without heart rate are not drawn; the caption counts them and the table lists them as **No heart rate**. A period without any heart rate shows an empty state that counts the recorded sessions.
- **Weekly climbing** shows elevation gain and, when Running is selected, running climb with the same date buckets and races, never two Y axes. The first stacks recorded elevation gain per sport, in metres or feet following the profile units, with the same sport order and colours as Training by sport; it lists only sports with a measured elevation in the period. Per sport, a week without sessions reads **No sessions recorded**, a session measured at zero reads `0 m`, a week where every session lacks elevation reads **Elevation missing**, and a partial week gives the known sum with the missing count. The second is running climb (m/km or ft/mi): the elevation gain divided by the distance of the runs that have both. It reads **No running** without running sessions, **Not measured** when no run has both, and **partial** when some runs were left out. Descent is not shown yet.
- **Resting heart rate and HRV** shows two charts, one per measure, never two Y axes: each night with a Garmin value is a dot, the 7-night average is a line (only when at least 4 of those 7 nights have a value), and a band shows the usual range. The usual range of a night is the average of the 60 nights before it plus or minus 0.75 standard deviation, shown only when at least 30 of those 60 nights have a value; otherwise the tooltip and table read **Not enough nights**. Nights without a value are gaps in the chart; the table lists every night of the chart, a night without a value reading **No measure** next to its 7-night average and usual range. When fewer than 7 nights were measured, the average says how many (`52 ms (5 of 7 nights)`). Nights after today read **Upcoming**. When Garmin marks a resting heart rate as possibly the lowest heart rate of the day, the table and tooltip read **(possible daily minimum)** and the caption counts those nights as possible daily minimums. A measure with no night in the period is not drawn; with neither, the page shows an empty state.
- **Estimated training load** is an estimate, labelled as such, not a measurement. Each recorded session's load is the Banister TRIMP: minutes × ΔHR × 0.64 × e^(1.92 × ΔHR), where ΔHR = (average heart rate − resting heart rate) / (maximum heart rate − resting heart rate), kept between 0 and 1. Resting and maximum heart rate come from **Settings → Profile & zones**; until both are set the page reads **Set your resting and maximum heart rate in Settings**. Each day of the period shows its summed load as a bar, with a 7-day average (shown from 4 days) and a 42-day average (shown from 24 days) as lines; the 42-day average reads sessions from 41 days before the period. Sessions are the Calendar's recorded sessions, so a manually logged workout counts as a session without heart rate. When an average uses fewer days than its window, it says how many (`81 (40 of 42 days)`). A day without sessions reads **No sessions recorded** and counts as zero in the averages. A day whose sessions all lack heart rate reads **No heart rate**, all lack duration **Duration missing**, and a mix of both **No heart rate or duration**; a day where only some do shows the known sum with the count of each. Both kinds of day are left out of the averages, never counted as zero, and the caption counts them. Days after today read **Upcoming**. The table lists every day of the chart. **No training in this period** replaces the chart only when no session falls in the period or the 41 days before it, so a rest week keeps the averages of earlier sessions.
- A period with no planned and no recorded session for the selected sports shows an empty state instead of empty axes.
- Choosing a period, sport selection or grouping shows only that selection's result. While it loads, the previous chart stays at reduced opacity; if the request fails, the chart is removed and an error with **Try again** replaces it. Opening or changing a chart reads only the chart: it does not reload the workspace or announce a save.
- **View values** opens a native table with the same date buckets, values and labels as the chart and tooltip. The chart has a legend, a caption describing the period, a tooltip that follows the pointer, and keyboard navigation (focus the chart, then the arrow keys).

## Observed implementation

The volume templates additionally use the training plugin's public `summarizeRange` calculation for daily/monthly buckets and the existing Calendar summary for weeks. [Bucket ranges](../../plugins/charts/bucket-ranges.ts) preserve the selected interval and clip months. Filtering operates on the canonical sport breakdown after session deduplication, including both planned and recorded totals. The original weekly response remains unfiltered for existing consumers; the additive `volume` projection contains the selected sports, grouping and dated buckets. Other templates receive `volume: null`. [Shared controls](../../plugins/charts/volume-controls.tsx) use existing Popover, Checkbox, Button and NativeSelect components; no shared design tokens or host training rules change.

The [manifest](../../plugins/charts/manifest.json) registers `/charts` and one server operation. [`summary`](../../plugins/charts/server.ts) validates the selection with the [public contract](../../packages/contracts/src/charts.ts), reads plans, workouts, events and the selected weeks' activities (from 41 days before the period for Estimated training load only) and, for Resting heart rate and HRV only, the health records from 60 days before the period in one read-only repeatable-read transaction (one consistent snapshot, without the domain write lock that saves and imports take), and maps each week through [the shared weekly calculation](../../plugins/charts/summary.ts). It returns weekly duration and elevation totals plus the selected volume buckets (overall and per chart sport, from the Calendar sport breakdown), the weekly running climb inputs (elevation and distance of the runs that have both, and how many runs there were), race dates and titles, for each recorded session of the period (activity or manual workout) its date, title, chart sport, average heart rate and duration, and the nightly resting heart rate and HRV from 60 days before the period (the usual range needs that history). The [page](../../plugins/charts/client.tsx) builds the volume bucket rows in [one place](../../plugins/charts/training-hours.ts) for the chart, the tooltip and the table, and loads the [chart](../../plugins/charts/training-hours-chart.tsx) and Recharts only when a template opens. [`useChartsSummary`](../../plugins/charts/use-charts-summary.ts) calls the operation directly rather than through the workspace save helper, and keys each result by the complete selection (template, date range, sports and grouping) and retry count, aborting superseded requests. [Training by sport](../../plugins/charts/training-by-sport.ts) adds the sport split to those rows; both charts share the [grouped frame](../../plugins/charts/grouped-chart.tsx).

The UI package adds the shadcn `chart` component on Recharts 3.10.1. Its dark selector follows `data-theme`, like the rest of the [shared UI](shared-ui.md). Series colours are plugin tokens in [charts CSS](../../plugins/charts/charts.css): a neutral graphite for recorded hours (not a sport colour, since the bar sums the selected sports) and the Calendar event amber for races. Sport colours are fixed per sport and checked with the palette validator in both themes; in the light theme cycling and swimming sit below 3:1 against the surface, so the legend and table carry their values. Other uses the neutral muted colour.

Evidence: unit, integration and browser tests linked above.
Limits: the general `GET /api/workspace` keeps its 121-day limit. Training load uses the Banister coefficient published for men (0.64 and 1.92) for every athlete, and average heart rate only: it misses effort that heart rate does not show, such as strength work, and the muscular cost of long descents. Accessibility checks are the keyboard and table browser tests; no automated axe scan runs, and text contrast inside the SVG is not measured automatically.

## Contracts and constraints

- Contract: [`charts.summary`](../../packages/contracts/src/operations.ts) input `{ template, weekStart, weeks, sports?, grouping? }` (weeks 1 to 53) and output `{ weeks, volume, races, sessions, nights, trainingLoadSessions }` (`trainingLoadSessions`: for Estimated training load only, the date, average heart rate and duration of each recorded session from 41 days before the period); each week carries `recordedSecondsBySport` and `recordedElevationMetersBySport` for the six chart sports, and `runningClimb` (`sessionCount`, `measuredSessionCount`, `elevationMeters`, `distanceMeters`).
- `sports` is a nonempty, unique subset of the six chart sport groups; `grouping` is day/week/month. Both are accepted only on volume templates and default to all/week. `volume.buckets` contains inclusive start/end dates, partial-month metadata, filtered planned/recorded duration totals, per-sport recorded duration/elevation totals and paired running-climb inputs. At most 371 daily buckets are returned.
- Architecture: [plugin boundaries](../architecture.md); the plugin depends on the public training calculation and has no schema or migration.
- Rationale: [ADR 007](../decisions/007-shared-shadcn-ui.md) for shared shadcn components.
