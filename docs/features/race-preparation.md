# Feature: Race preparation

## Purpose and scope

The athlete keeps one plan per race: what they want from it, a briefing and the effort to hold on each section of the course. An external assistant can write the strategy; the owner reviews it before anything is saved. Settings keeps the nutrition products the athlete trains and races with, how much they can carry and their diet preferences, reused by every race.

Outside scope: scenarios, the post-race review, a list of past versions and watch export.

## Intended behavior

Specification status: draft. Covers the stored race plan, its commands, agent proposals and the Preparation, Race day, Nutrition and Race card tabs of the Races page.

Requirement source: [issue #121](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/issues/121) and the decisions proposed on it on 2026-11-16 (one document per race with no version list, proposals stay immutable, assumptions and open questions travel with the proposal, one permission per race). Record: [decision 023](../decisions/023-race-plan-record.md).

- A race plan belongs to one race, an event of kind race, and a race has at most one plan. The race of a plan never changes.
- A plan holds a target of up to 120 characters such as "Finish comfortably", a briefing of up to 4000 characters, pacing, assumptions and open questions (up to 20 lines of 300 characters each).
- Pacing is a list of up to 200 sections in course order. Each section runs from one distance to a greater one, in meters from the start line, and carries an effort of up to 120 characters. Sections cannot overlap; one may start where the previous ends.
- Only the owner sets the target. An assistant writes the briefing, pacing, assumptions and open questions and leaves the target as it is.
- A plan remembers what its strategy was written for: the course it used and the target at that time. The owner changing the target alone leaves that memory untouched, so the plan can be shown as needing review; saving with a course named again refreshes it.
- Deleting a race deletes its plan in the same change, and one undo restores both. An event with a plan cannot become a goal or another kind.
- There is no command to delete a plan alone.
- A plan also holds race-day timing, set by the owner, or by an assistant connected for the race: a start time (HH:MM), a finish cutoff and up to 60 aid stations. A cutoff is a number of days after race day (0 to 6) and an HH:MM time. Times are wall-clock times in the profile timezone; a race in another timezone and a clock change during the race are not handled. A plan without them has no start time, no finish cutoff and no station.
- An aid station has a name of up to 80 characters, a distance from the start line, an optional cutoff, a planned stop of up to 24 hours, water or not, food offered (up to 200 characters), a drop bag of up to 20 distinct products, each with 1 to 100 servings (empty means no drop bag), an action (up to 300 characters), where the information comes from (up to 120 characters) and whether it is confirmed. Stations run in course order, each farther than the one before, each with its own id.
- An update that leaves the start time, finish cutoff or stations out keeps them; the Preparation form never sends them. An assistant connected for this race writes them through [race plan proposals](agent-training.md#race-plan-proposals).
- `plugins/race-analysis/race-day.ts` computes arrivals from the plan and the course estimate; nothing it returns is stored. Each 5 km section's estimated time is spread along the section by distance; the planning range scales with the estimate's low and high ends. The margin is the cutoff minus the latest arrival. The estimate is the moving time, and the planned stops of stations on the course are added to it: the arrival at a station counts the stops of the stations before it, never its own or a later one, and the finish counts every stop on the course. Each leg between stations has its distance, its climbing spread by distance (unknown when a section has no elevation) and its moving time. A station beyond the course is flagged and has no arrival. Without an estimate only cutoffs and distances remain; without a start time arrivals are times from the start, with no clock time or margin.
- **Races** shows five tabs for the selected race, **Course**, **Preparation**, **Race day**, **Nutrition** and **Race card**. Preparation edits the target, the briefing, one effort per section, assumptions and open questions (one per line), and works without an assistant: a plan can be saved with the target alone.
- Pacing rows are the planned sections plus the 5 km course sections no planned section covers. A row left blank is not saved. Without a course and without planned sections, the tab points to the Course tab.
- A plan with something written needs review when the course in use is no longer the one it was written for, or when the target changed since. Preparation then says so and offers **Mark as reviewed**, which changes nothing else. While the form has unsaved changes, it asks to save them first. Saving a change to the briefing, pacing, assumptions or open questions also records the course in use; saving the target alone does not.
- **Race day** opens with `Start 07:00 → finish 13:15` (or `Finish 6h 15m after the start` without a start time), the planning range and `5h 57m moving, 18m stops`; planned stops are added to the moving time. Then comes the finish cutoff with its margin (`Finish cutoff 15:00 · <time> after the latest finish`, or `Latest finish is <time> after the cutoff` in the danger color), or `No finish cutoff`. Without an estimate only the finish cutoff and the start time remain. It recomputes everything from the saved plan and the saved course estimate each time it is shown; an unsaved terrain draft on Course does not change it.
- **Change start time** opens **Start and finish cutoff**: a start time and a finish cutoff (day and time), each optional. A cutoff day without a time is not saved: the cutoff is cleared.
- **Change effort and weather** opens **Effort and weather**: an effort and a weather percentage (steps of 0.5, effort -30 to 50, weather -20 to 50) and a weather note of up to 300 characters, the athlete's own assumption and never a forecast. The window starts from the plan as it was when opened, sends only the fields that changed and sends nothing when none did. Race day, Nutrition and the race card use one adjusted estimate: moving time and its planning range are multiplied by `(1 + effort/100) × (1 + weather/100)`, and planned stops are added unchanged. A plan at 0 and 0 shows exactly the times it showed before. When either value is not 0, Race day and the race card (and its downloaded file) read `Effort +8 % · weather +6 % (your assumption)` followed by the note, and Course keeps the unadjusted finish with "Before effort and weather". Saving the first time creates the plan, written for the course in use. Neither value makes the plan need review.
- **Aid stations** lists the stations in course order, each with its distance, water, food, the number of drop bag items, **Not confirmed** or **Confirmed · <source>**, the arrival range and aim, the cutoff and its margin (`<time> margin`, or `<time> after the cutoff` in the danger color), the planned stop and what to do. A leg row before each station, and one to the finish, gives its distance, its climbing when every section has elevation, and its moving time; a leg starting at 0 m starts at **Start**. A second line gives what to carry when leaving: `carry <litres> L water, <servings> × <product>`, followed by `, over capacity` when the water needed until the next water is more than the carry capacity. An unknown amount is left out of the line; with nothing known there is no carry line. Up to 900 px each station becomes a card, with **Edit** and **Remove** 44 px high and 8 px apart. A station beyond the course shows **Beyond the course** and **No arrival**.
- **Add station** and **Edit** open one window: name, distance in the profile's units, cutoff day and time, planned stop in minutes, what the station offers (water, food), the drop bag (a row per product from Settings with whole servings up to 100, each with **Remove**, up to 20 rows, plus **Add drop bag item**), what to do, and where this comes from (source, confirmed). Saving puts the station in its place by distance; a second station at the same distance is refused before saving with "Another aid station is already at this distance." Saving an edit starts from the station as saved now. Without products in Settings the window says "Add products in Settings, then Nutrition, to fill a drop bag." A product removed from Settings stays selected as "Removed from Settings" until it is changed or removed. **Remove** asks to confirm with **Remove station**. The first station or start time creates the plan, written for the course in use.
- Race day states, with their exact text:
  - No station: **No aid stations yet**, "Add the stations from the race guide: distance, cutoff and what they offer. Nothing is filled in for you." and **Add the first station**.
  - No estimate: **Arrival times are unknown**, the course's reasons, "Cutoffs and stops still show.", "Carry amounts need leg times, so they are hidden." and **Add past efforts on Course**, which opens the Course tab.
  - No start time: "Set a start time to see arrival clock times and cutoff margins." Arrivals show as times after the start, with no margin.
  - Needs review: an alert titled **This plan needs review**, "The course or your target changed after this plan was written. Check that the stations still hold." and **Mark as reviewed**. Preparation shows the same alert with its own sentence. A plan with stations alone counts as written, so both tabs show it. Saving a station does not clear it.
  - Save failed: the window stays open with the draft, the server's message, "Use Reload saved data to review the saved version. Your draft will stay open." and **Reload saved data**. After the reload, saving applies the station to the saved plan.
- **What to carry** is computed when shown, never stored, from the timed legs, the nutrition schedule and targets, the products and carry capacity in Settings and the drop bags:
  - Each schedule row is taken every `everyMinutes` of moving time, the first intake after one interval. Servings add up along the course and are rounded up on the running total, so the legs add up to the rounded total.
  - Products come back only from drop bags. Leaving the start, each product covers every leg until the next drop bag holding it, or the finish. At a drop bag, what is left plus the bag is compared with the legs until the next bag holding it: a smaller amount reads `<n> × <product> short until <place>`, a larger one stays carried into the next legs. A drop bag product outside the schedule is listed only.
  - Water uses the fluid target, else the schedule's hourly fluid, else it is unknown. Bottles are filled at the start and at stations with water, with what the legs until the next water need, up to the carry capacity. Over a known carry capacity (0 included), the block shows **One leg is over your carrying capacity** (or **<n> legs are over…**) and "<from> → <to> needs about <litres> L at <n> ml per hour. You carry up to <litres> L." An unknown capacity is never compared.
  - Food offered at a station replaces the earliest intake of each food product in the next leg, once even when the schedule lists the product twice; nothing is added to the carbohydrate.
  - Unconfirmed stations are counted like confirmed ones; their row already says **Not confirmed**.
  - Amounts to carry show only on the leg rows. The block shows the capacity alert and, for each drop bag, **<station> drop bag** with its contents and its shortfalls. Other lines, as facts: "Add a food plan on Nutrition to see what to carry." without a schedule; "Water need unknown: no fluid target and no fluid value in the food plan."; "Carry capacity unknown: water is not checked against it." Products without a fluid value and products removed from Settings are left out; the Nutrition tab already says so. **How this is worked out**, folded, repeats these rules. Without leg times (no estimate) the block and the carry lines are hidden.
- A save over a plan changed or created elsewhere is refused and offers **Reload saved data**. The draft stays open on the version it started from, and saving it again is refused until **Use saved plan** replaces it with the saved plan. The fields are read-only while a save and the reload after it are in progress.
- Under the form, **Weeks until race day** lists each week from the current one to race week, until race day, with its phase, its weekly duration target and the planned sessions. It is read-only and links to the calendar, where phases and workouts are edited.
- When Agent Bridge is enabled, the tab links to the Agent workspace, where a connection is limited to the race and proposals are reviewed. The tab shows no proposal status of its own.
- Assistants read and propose a race plan only through a planning connection limited to that race. Details in [Agent training](agent-training.md#race-plan-proposals).

### Nutrition in the race plan

- A plan holds a nutrition part, set by the owner or by an assistant connected for the race: targets per hour (carbohydrate 0 to 500 g, fluid 0 to 5000 ml, sodium 0 to 10000 mg, each unknown until set), a schedule of up to 30 rows (a product of the profile, servings from more than 0 to 100, fractions allowed, every 1 to 1440 minutes), up to 20 cues (when, up to 80 characters, and what, up to 300) and three free texts of up to 4000 characters: days before, breakfast and recovery. A plan saved before this reads with unknown targets, no schedule, no cue and empty texts.
- An update replaces the nutrition part as a whole; an update that leaves it out keeps it. An assistant connected for this race writes it as a whole through race plan proposals.
- A schedule row names a product by its id and is not checked against the profile: a product removed from Settings stays in the schedule and is flagged when the plan is shown. A proposal from an assistant is refused when a schedule row or drop bag names a product that is not in the profile. The hourly intake adds, for each nutrient, the known values of the scheduled products (servings × value × 60 / minutes); a product without the value is left out of that total and named; a total with no known value is unknown, never 0.
- A plan written without a target does not need review when a target is set later; a plan written for a target needs review when that target changes or is cleared.
- **Nutrition** opens on **During the race**, with **Days before**, **Breakfast** and **Recovery** beside it in one segmented control. Each phase text has **Edit**, which opens a window with one text field; an empty phase reads "Nothing written yet." **During the race** starts with "Your plan, not a prescription" and "Targets and quantities are what you agreed and tested."
- **Targets per hour** shows carbohydrate large (per hour moving), then fluid and sodium (per hour); an unset target reads "unknown". With no target set it shows **No hourly targets yet** and **Set targets** instead. **Edit targets** and **Set targets** open a window where an empty field means unknown. Every save sends the whole nutrition part with the plan's revision, and the first one creates the plan, written for the course in use.
- **During the race** lists, under the targets, each schedule row (`Every <n> min`, `Every hour`, or `Every <h>h <mm>m` beyond an hour, `<servings> × <product>`, the known values one intake brings, and in the **Tolerance** column the product's tolerance from Settings, colored as in Settings) and then each cue, with nothing in the **Tolerance** column. **Edit plan** opens one window with a row per product (a product from Settings, servings above 0 and up to 100, every 1 to 1440 minutes) and per cue (when and what to do, both required and not blank), each with **Remove**, plus **Add product** (up to 30) and **Add cue** (up to 20). Adding a row moves the focus to its first field; removing one moves it to the matching add button. Without products in Settings the window says "Add products in Settings, then Nutrition, to plan them here." and only cues can be added.
- With a schedule, each target of **Targets per hour** also reads what the schedule plans per hour, with the gap to the target: `planned 50 g, 17% below`, `planned 75 g, 25% above`, `planned 60 g, on target`, `planned 50 g` without a target or with a target of 0, or `planned unknown` when no scheduled product has the value. Under the rows, with a course estimate and a known carbohydrate intake: "About <n> g carbohydrate over <time> moving." using the moving time of Race day; a moving time of 0 still counts. No advice is written for the athlete.
- States, with their exact text: no products in Settings and nothing planned: **Add the products you use**, "Gels, drinks and food live in Settings so every race can reuse them. Targets work without them. Open Settings, then Nutrition."; products but nothing planned: "Nothing planned during the race yet."; products without a value: one notice, **Not counted in the hourly totals**, naming each product with its missing values (`Citrus gel (no fluid value), Salted crackers (no carbohydrate or fluid value)`) and "Add the label values in Settings to count them."; a product removed from Settings: the row reads `<servings> × Removed product`, and **A product of this plan was removed from Settings** with "It is left out of the hourly totals. Edit the plan to replace it." In the window, a removed product stays selected as "Removed from Settings" until it is changed or removed.
- **Your preferences**, folded, shows the first line of the preferences from Settings beside its title; opened, all of them, or "No preferences in Settings yet.", and says to open Settings, then Nutrition, to change them.
- A plan whose nutrition holds anything counts as written for the needs-review rule. Nutrition then shows "The course or your target changed after this plan was written. Check that the nutrition plan still holds." and **Mark as reviewed**.
- A save over a plan changed elsewhere keeps the window open with the server's message and **Reload saved data**; after the reload, saving applies the change to the saved plan.

### Race card

- **Race card**, the last tab, puts the race day on one page to read on a phone, print or keep offline. It is computed from the saved plan, the saved course estimate and the profile each time it is shown; nothing is stored for it. Without a plan, or with a plan that has neither an aid station nor anything on Nutrition, it shows **No race card yet** and "The card is built from the aid stations on Race day and the food plan on Nutrition. Add either one to see it here."
- The card shows, each block only when it has something: **Expected finish** (a clock time, or a time after the start without a start time) with its planning range, **Keep in mind** (the briefing), **Breakfast**, then one row for the start, each aid station in course order and the finish, **During the race** (the cues), a **Food** line and **Recovery**. The days before are not on the card. A row has its name and distance with the arrival range on the same line, then on separate lines what to do, `Leave with <water>, <servings> × <product>` (followed by `, over capacity`, in the danger color, when the water until the next water is more than the carry capacity; an unknown amount is left out), the cutoff with its margin (`Cutoff 09:30 · 5m margin`, or `· 5m after the cutoff` in the danger color, also bold and underlined in print), and in small type the planned stop, the next leg (distance, climbing, moving time), `not confirmed` and `beyond the course`. The finish row has no arrival range: the summary has it. The **Food** line reads `50 g carbohydrate per hour, 500 ml fluid per hour: 1 × <product>, every 30 min`; unset targets are left out, and a product removed from Settings reads "Removed product".
- Under the title the card says what it was written for (`Written for <course file> · target <target>`, `an earlier course` when the course changed, `no course` when none, no target part when the plan was written without one) and `plan revision <n>`. The plan has no save date.
- Without a start time the times are counted from the start, said once: "Times are counted from the start." The card gives at most one warning, the first that applies: "No finish estimate yet: arrival times and carry amounts are not shown."; "Water to carry is unknown: no fluid target and no fluid in the food plan."; "Carry capacity is unknown: water is not checked against it."; "No food plan yet: products to carry are not shown." A plan that needs review shows the alert of the other tabs, "The course or your target changed after this plan was written. Check that the card still holds.", with **Mark as reviewed**.
- **Print** opens the browser print dialog. In print the page keeps only the card: no menu, tabs, race selector or buttons, black on white, a station never split across pages; a plan that needs review prints "This plan needs review: the course or the target changed after it was written." under the title.
- **Download offline copy** saves `<race title>-race-card.html` (accents and symbols removed, `race` when nothing is left), one HTML file with its style inside, no script, no link and no image, readable without the application. Every typed text is escaped. It carries the same blocks, the plan revision, the date of the download in the browser's clock (`Saved copy downloaded on <date> from plan revision <n>. It does not update: if the plan changes, download it again.`) and, when the plan needed review at that moment, "This plan needed review when this copy was downloaded: the course or the target changed after it was written." A copy older than the plan is recognized by comparing its revision with the one on the card.

### Fueling rehearsals

- A fueling rehearsal records, for one race and one session of the calendar, what was planned and taken, how the stomach felt, the conditions and what to keep or change. A race may have several rehearsals; a session has at most one per race, and may serve two races.
- It holds up to 50 products, each with its name, carbohydrate and fluid per serving as they were when logged (removing or editing the product in Settings does not change them), the servings planned (unknown without a plan) and the servings taken (0 to 100, halves allowed). It also holds the plain water taken (0 to 20000 ml), the stomach (comfortable, mild discomfort or poor, the tolerance levels of Settings without "not tested"), the conditions (up to 120 characters) and the lesson (up to 4000 characters).
- A rehearsal can only be logged on a session marked completed or partial; a skipped or pending session is refused. Only the owner logs, edits and deletes one; assistants cannot, directly or by deleting its session.
- Its race and session never change. Editing it later is allowed even when the session is no longer marked completed. Logging it never changes the session, its completion or its imported activity.
- **Nutrition** lists, under **During the race**, the **Fueling rehearsals** of the race, newest first: the date the session was performed (or "No date" once its completion is cleared), the session and its duration; on a second line the carbohydrate taken per hour (the total without a duration, "carbohydrate unknown" when no taken product has a value), the fluid in litres ("fluid unknown" when a taken product has no fluid value), "<n> products not counted" when taken products have no carbohydrate value, and the conditions; the lesson on its own line, cut to one line; "Session no longer marked completed" when it is; and the stomach, coloured like the tolerance badges of Settings. One message shows at a time: "Add products to During the race first." when the race's food plan holds no product from Settings, or "Complete a session in the last 12 weeks to log a fueling rehearsal." when no session can be logged, with **Log a fueling rehearsal** disabled in both cases; otherwise "No fueling rehearsal logged yet." when there is none.
- **Log a fueling rehearsal** opens a window "For <race>. Your recorded activity stays as imported." It offers the sessions completed or partial in the last 12 weeks that have no rehearsal for this race, newest first. A row per product of the race's food plan, in the plan's order, shows the servings planned: whole intakes over the session's recorded duration, the first one after its interval (`floor(duration / every) × servings`, added up when a product is scheduled twice), "unknown" without a duration. A removed product is left out. **Plain water** reads "Not planned". Each taken field shows its unit (servings or ml) and is required, so an empty one is never counted as 0: until all are filled the window says "Enter what you took, 0 if none." and shows no total. Then "Taken: <n> g carbohydrate per hour · <n> L fluid" (the total without a duration, "carbohydrate unknown" or "fluid unknown" as in the list) and one notice naming the products left out for an unknown carbohydrate value. Then the stomach (Comfortable, Mild discomfort, Poor), the conditions, "What would you keep or change?" and "Saved with this race. An assistant connected for this race can read fueling rehearsals.".
- **Edit** opens the same window with the session locked and the products, values and planned servings as logged. **Delete rehearsal**, in the row of **Cancel** and **Save changes**, asks to confirm with **Delete rehearsal**. A save over a rehearsal changed elsewhere is refused with the server's message and **Reload saved data**; after the reload, saving applies the draft to the saved rehearsal.
- Deleting the race, the session (once its completion is cleared) or the plan holding the session deletes the rehearsal in the same change, and one undo restores it. The confirmation says so: "Delete this event and its fueling rehearsals?", "Delete this event, its race plan and its fueling rehearsals?", "Delete “<session>” and its fueling rehearsals?" and "Delete “<plan>”, its planned sessions and their fueling rehearsals?". An undo that would bring back a rehearsal whose session is gone is refused and changes nothing.

### Race scenarios

- A race scenario stores one alternative to the race plan for a race that has a plan: a name (1 to 80 characters after trimming), an effort and weather assumption like the plan's, the stop at each aid station in seconds (0 to 24 hours, each station once, up to 60), and what it was made from: the course, the plan's stations (ids, distances and stops, up to 60), effort and weather. A scenario stored before the stops, effort and weather were kept reads without them. A race holds at most four.
- Only the owner creates, edits and deletes scenarios. Assistants cannot write them and never read them: they are not in any planning context.
- Deleting a race deletes its scenarios; the confirmation names them and one undo restores them. Undoing the deletion of a scenario is refused when the race already holds four others.
- This release stores the record only; no screen shows scenarios yet.

### Nutrition in Settings

Requirement source: [issue #122](https://github.com/bidulelabs/athlete-panel/issues/122), first part.

- The athlete profile holds an optional `nutrition`: up to 50 products, a carry capacity in millilitres and diet preferences of up to 2000 characters. A profile saved before this has no `nutrition` and reads as before.
- A product has a name, a type (gel, drink, food or other), its carbohydrate, fluid, sodium and caffeine per serving, where the values come from (the label or the athlete's estimate) and the athlete's tolerance (comfortable, mild discomfort, poor or not tested). Each value may be unknown and has a maximum (500 g carbohydrate, 5000 ml fluid, 10000 mg sodium, 1000 mg caffeine). Product ids are unique.
- **Settings** gains a **Nutrition** tab. Products are listed in a table, as cards below 760 px with caffeine in the grey line when above 0; an unknown value reads "unknown", a zero reads "0". Tolerance badges are coloured: comfortable as success, mild discomfort and poor as warnings. **Add product** and **Edit** open a dialog; saving it, or confirming **Remove**, saves the product list at once. **Save capacity and preferences** saves the rest.
- Before the first profile save, the tab says **Save your profile first** and offers no editing, so nutrition never creates a profile with default settings.
- A save over a profile changed elsewhere is refused and offers **Reload saved data**; the draft stays until **Load saved nutrition** replaces it. Saving an edit to a product removed elsewhere says "This product was removed elsewhere." and saves nothing. Saving **Profile & zones** leaves nutrition unchanged.
- An assistant reads the profile's nutrition only through a planning connection limited to a race, whether or not that connection includes private notes. A race grant approved with `includeRehearsals`, which the approval screen sets, also covers that race's fueling rehearsals; one approved earlier does not until it is approved again. `includeNotes` does not blank their `lesson` or `conditions`. Other planning connections and historical review receive the profile without the `nutrition` key, so their context digest does not depend on it. Saving nutrition still changes the profile revision, so like any profile change it asks a connection that already read its context to read it again (`409 stale_context`).

## Observed implementation

Implementation status: implemented in the working tree.

Training stores race plans as records of kind `racePlan`, beside plans, workouts, notes, events and phases. The engine checks the race of each plan, keeps one plan per race and removes a plan with its race. Fueling rehearsals are records of kind `rehearsal`, handled in `plugins/training/engine/handlers/rehearsal.ts`, listed by `plugins/race-analysis/rehearsal-list.tsx` and logged in `rehearsal-dialog.tsx`, with planned servings, the sessions offered and the taken totals in `rehearsal.ts`; the engine checks their race, their session and one rehearsal per session and race, and removes them with their race, session or plan. The Preparation tab is `plugins/race-analysis/preparation.tsx` and the Race day tab is `plugins/race-analysis/race-day-tab.tsx` with its windows in `race-day-dialogs.tsx`; the Nutrition tab is `nutrition-tab.tsx` with its windows in `nutrition-dialogs.tsx` and the schedule rows and totals in `nutrition-schedule.tsx`, and the hourly intake is in `nutrition.ts`; which rows it shows, when a plan needs review, how a station form is read and which command a save sends are in `plugins/race-analysis/race-plan-form.ts`; arrivals, margins, legs and moving time are in `plugins/race-analysis/race-day.ts`; the Race card tab is `race-card-tab.tsx`, its rows and texts are built by `race-card.ts` and the offline file by `race-card-file.ts`, and the amounts to carry come from `carry.ts`. Race analysis exposes the course summary through `plugins/race-analysis/public/server.ts`; `instance/race-context.ts` hands it to Agent Bridge only when Race analysis is enabled. Agent Bridge adds the plan of the granted race to the planning context and checks each proposed race plan against the grant.

## Contracts and constraints

- `racePlan.create` and `racePlan.update` are owner commands and agent proposal commands; the agent form has no target and requires `courseId`.
- The agent forms also write `startTime`, `finishCutoff`, `stations` and `nutrition`, each replaced as a whole. They carry no `source` or `verified`: the engine keeps both of a station by its id, clears `verified` when the agent changes its distance or name, and gives a new station `verified: false` and an empty `source`. A drop bag or food schedule product must be in the nutrition profile or already in the saved plan, or the proposal is refused with `unknown_product`. Record: [decision 024](../decisions/024-race-day-in-race-plan.md).
- `racePlan.create` and `racePlan.update` by the owner also accept `effortPercent` (-30 to 50), `weatherPercent` (-20 to 50), both in steps of 0.5, and `weatherNote` (up to 300 characters after trimming); omitted, an update keeps each value and a create stores 0, 0 and an empty note, as a plan stored before reads. The agent forms have no such fields and `boundedContext` leaves them out of `racePlans`, so no context digest depends on them. Upgrade and rollback: [race effort upgrade](../development.md#race-effort-upgrade).
- Modern planning scopes carry optional `raceId`. The planning context has a `racePlans` key only when it is set.
- No SQL migration. Upgrade and rollback: [race plan upgrade](../development.md#race-plan-upgrade), [race day upgrade](../development.md#race-day-upgrade) and [race nutrition upgrade](../development.md#race-nutrition-upgrade).
- `raceScenario.create`, `raceScenario.update` and `raceScenario.delete` are owner commands only; the update never changes `eventId`. A workspace stored without scenarios reads as having none. No SQL migration: [race scenario upgrade](../development.md#race-scenario-upgrade).
- `rehearsal.create`, `rehearsal.update` and `rehearsal.delete` are owner commands only. A planning context limited to a race and approved with `includeRehearsals` includes that race's rehearsals, in full whether or not it includes private notes; other contexts omit the `rehearsals` key. No SQL migration. Upgrade and rollback: [rehearsal upgrade](../development.md#rehearsal-upgrade).
- `profile.set` accepts `nutrition`, replaced as a whole. The Nutrition tab is `plugins/athlete/nutrition-settings.tsx`; Agent Bridge leaves `nutrition` out of the profile in `boundedContext` without `raceId`; Athlete's `historicalProfile` always leaves it out, and the history response contract has no `nutrition` key. No SQL migration. Upgrade and rollback: [nutrition profile upgrade](../development.md#nutrition-profile-upgrade).
